# Channelflow

This demo shows the inbox-first Channelflow product: one queue for four
channels (WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Email, TikTok). Four screens are wired:
**Inbox** (default — channel filter chips, thread list with channel-coloured
circular avatars, search, and a conversation panel with a transcript and a
quick-reply composer that sends on Cmd/Ctrl+Enter), **Bookings** (sortable,
filterable table that opens a side-sheet with party / channel / guide /
value), **Commission** (a per-tour-guide ledger that auto-applies 10%
commission to confirmed bookings with a party of 6+), and **Analytics**
(channel mix, conversion, and the next 7 days of confirmed tours).

All names, IDs, and codes are synthetic placeholders (`Guest 01`…`Guest 50`,
`BK-0001`…`BK-0030`, `TG-01`…`TG-08`, `CM-0001`…`CM-0025`); channel labels
(WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Email, TikTok) are public product names used as
category badges only. Brand identity, layout conventions, and component
vocabulary are derived from the production app's visual style — no
production code, schemas, integrations, or fixtures are committed here.
